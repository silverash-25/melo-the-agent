## Interpretation
The startup's monthly revenue shows considerable variation with a standard deviation of approximately $27,565. The median revenue ($50,901) is slightly higher than the mean ($49,507), indicating a relatively symmetric distribution with a slight left skew. The maximum revenue of nearly $100,000 represents a strong peak performance month.

Data was generated via Python script creating 50 random monthly revenue data points and saved to `revenue.csv`. Analysis performed using the data analysis tool.
